Getting around Whitehorse

Before you go: ride notes

In this guide

PlanningAboutQuestionsEnquiriesSeasonsCostsTrip questions

Start with your priorities

A practical guide to planning a ride across town. Know what to ask, what to share and what to confirm.

Pin down the pickup

Share the entrance or landmark as well as the address. Agree on how the driver will contact you.

Size up the trip

Say how many people and bags are travelling. Discuss mobility equipment or child-seat needs in advance.

Confirm the fare basis

Ask whether the trip is metered or quoted, how waiting is handled and which payment methods are accepted.

Take the questions with you

Use the trip checklist to keep the important questions together. Confirm the answers directly with your chosen provider before making a commitment.
