Common Questions

How do I get a taxi in Whitehorse?

Contact us to request a pickup. Pre-booking is recommended for early morning and airport trips. On-demand service is available but not guaranteed during peak times.

Do you accept credit cards?

We accept cash and major credit cards. Payment is due at the end of the ride.

How much does a taxi cost?

Standard city rides within Whitehorse have a base fare plus per-km rate. Contact us for a quote to your destination. Long-distance trips are priced by arrangement.

Can I book for a specific time?

Yes. Pre-booking is the best way to guarantee availability, especially for early morning airport trips or late-night returns.

Do you serve areas outside Whitehorse?

Yes. We provide service to Haines Junction, Carcross, Tagish, Marsh Lake, and other Yukon communities. Contact us for pricing.

Are your drivers licensed?

All our drivers hold valid Yukon driver's licenses and have undergone background checks. Safety is our priority.
